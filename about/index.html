about rapsap

the hybrid supermarket.

kiranas offer proximity but not assortment. quick-commerce offers speed but not experience. rapsap was built to be both — a premium physical store with its own quick delivery.

two halves of one shop.

india's essentials retail is at an inflection point. customers want the trust and experience of a premium store and the convenience of online delivery. most formats give you one or the other.

rapsap brings both together: high-quality ambience, curated assortment and clear category signage in store — seamlessly integrated with an app for quick fulfilment. premium experience, everyday value, closer home.
