about rapsap

the hybrid
supermarket.

kiranas offer proximity but not assortment. quick-commerce offers speed but not experience. rapsap was built to be both — a premium physical store with its own online quick delivery.

why rapsap exists

two halves of one shop.

India's essentials retail is at an inflection point. customers want the trust and experience of a premium store and the convenience of online delivery. most formats give you one or the other.

rapsap brings both together: high-quality ambience, curated assortment and clear category signage in store — seamlessly integrated with an app for quick fulfilment. premium experience, everyday value, closer home.

how we operate

our operating principles.

freshness

produce checked and rotated daily; nothing stays on the shelf past its best.

hygiene

one cleanliness standard, audited across every store, every shift.

smart pricing

speed

2024

the model proves out

stores expand across high-density Thane & Mumbai townships; café and bakery added in-store.

2025

app & quick delivery

the Rapsap app launches — the full aisle, store-backed, delivered quickly.

2026
